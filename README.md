## **Introduction**

Public health clinics across South Africa often experience overcrowding, resulting in long waiting times and limited visibility for patients. Many patients wait for hours without knowing their position in the queue, which can lead to frustration and missed care.

**SD CareQueue** is a web-based system designed to improve patient experience and clinic efficiency by enabling appointment booking, virtual queue management, and real-time updates.

---

## **Project Objectives**

This project follows **Agile methodology**, incorporates **CI/CD principles**, and applies a **test-driven development (TDD)** approach to build a scalable and reliable system.

The system aims to:

* Reduce patient waiting time and uncertainty
* Improve clinic workflow and patient flow management
* Provide data-driven insights through analytics

---

## **Core Features**

### **1. Appointment Booking**

* View available time slots at nearby clinics
* Book, reschedule, or cancel appointments
* Join a virtual queue as a walk-in patient

---

### **2. Queue Management**

* Staff manage daily queues
* Update patient statuses (Waiting, In Consultation, Complete)
* Add or reschedule patients

---

### **3. Real-Time Patient Tracking**

* View queue position and estimated wait time
* Receive notifications when your turn is approaching

---

### **4. Clinic Directory (SA Data Integration)**

* Real clinic data sourced from South African health datasets
* Filter by province, district, facility type, and services

---

### **5. Clinic & Staff Management**

* Admins manage clinics, services, and staff
* Staff set their availability

---

### **6. Notifications & Reminders**

* Appointment reminders
* Queue updates and alerts

---

### **7. Analytics & Reporting**

* Average wait times by clinic and time of day
* Appointment no-show rates
* Custom dashboard views
* Export reports as CSV or PDF
